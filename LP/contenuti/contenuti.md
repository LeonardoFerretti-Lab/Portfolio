# Progetti UX/UI

### 1. Demo IT
- **Tipo:** Redesign sito web + logo
- **Descrizione:** For the UX/UI project for Demo IT SRL, I redesigned the company website to create a more intuitive, clear, and brand-aligned user experience. A heuristic analysis identified opportunities to improve usability, visual hierarchy, and content communication, with particular attention to mobile responsiveness, readability, and effective calls to action. In parallel, a modern logo was developed using fluid geometric shapes to convey connection, innovation, and reliability. The result is a minimalist, professional, and high-tech design that enhances functionality, visual appeal, and user engagement while supporting the company's communication and conversion goals.
- **Goals:** Innovative and high-tech design · Modern and consistent visual identity · Client education and guidance
- **Immagini:** `assets/images/ux-ui/ux-ui-03.webp`

### 2. Naive Shore
- **Tipo:** Branding + UX/UI (e-commerce tavole da surf artigianali)
- **Descrizione:** Naive Shore is a comprehensive branding and UX/UI design project for a handcrafted surfboard brand. The work includes the development of a cohesive visual identity, featuring a custom logo, bespoke board graphics, and a color palette aligned with the brand's core values: craftsmanship, authenticity, and a relaxed lifestyle. The website and social media presence were designed to showcase the community, highlight visual content, and provide a simple yet immersive digital experience. The project combines aesthetics, storytelling, and functionality to create an engaging and accessible.
- **Goals:** Welcoming and reassuring style · Brand identity system · Brand awareness e community
- **Immagini:** `assets/images/ux-ui/ux-ui-04.webp`

### 3. Tax Advisory Firm – Studio tributario Lupi & Associated
- **Tipo:** Sito one-page
- **Descrizione:** The website was designed as a one-page experience, simplifying navigation and guiding users through a clear, continuous journey that facilitates an immediate understanding of the firm's identity and services. Marble serves as the primary visual element, evoking concepts of structural solidity while conveying stability and reliability. At the same time, its refined texture introduces an elegant and institutional aesthetic, reflecting the authority of the legal profession and reinforcing perceptions credibility, and professionalism. The design balances clarity and visual sophistication, creating a trustworthy digital presence aligned with the values of a tax advisory firm.
- **Goals:** Strong online presence · Clarity and elegance · Authority within a single page
- **Immagini:** `assets/images/ux-ui/ux-ui-05.webp`

### 4. Landing Page – Weopera
- **Tipo:** Landing page di conversione
- **Descrizione:** I designed the landing page for Weopera, an agency specializing in web development, eCommerce, and SEO strategies, with the goal of improving user conversion and clearly communicating the value of its services. The creative concept revolves around the theme of space, used as a metaphor for the digital growth journey: clients begin in a state of uncertainty and are guided toward measurable results through the agency's expertise. The landing page was conceived as a progressive narrative funnel, carefully structured to lead users from initial curiosity to requesting a consultation and engaging with the brand.
- **Goals:** Converting users into leads · Innovative design · Visual style aligned with the website
- **Immagini:** `assets/images/ux-ui/ux-ui-06.webp`

# Progetti di illustrazione

### 5. Six Memos for the Next Millennium (Lezioni americane – Italo Calvino)
- **Tipo:** Libro illustrato / ricerca artistica
- **Descrizione:** The project is inspired by Italo Calvino's essay, is an experimental artistic research, exploring the visual translation of the concepts of lightness, speed, exactness, visibility, and multiplicity through illustration. The project develops synthetic and atmospheric visuals based on geometric forms and essential compositions. The geometric language defines both the illustrations and the editorial layout, creating a coherent visual system. The work investigates the relationship between literature and image, transforming abstract concepts into contemporary visual narratives that merge conceptual thinking with visual clarity and structured design.
- **Goals:** Visually translating the concepts of the essay · Developing evocative digital illustrations · Maintaining a coherent graphic and editorial style
- **Immagini:** `assets/images/lezioni-americane/` (11 immagini) + slide `illustration-portfolio-03/04.webp`

### 6. Contest Calendart
- **Tipo:** Illustrazione vettoriale per concorso
- **Descrizione:** Created for a design contest, the illustration employs a modern vector visual language to celebrate office work, elevating everyday desk routines through the ironic transformation of a stationery object into a symbol of speed, innovation, and design excellence. In the foreground, a designer sketches the motorcycle's technical details on a drawing board while, above him, his idea visually takes shape and materializes. The vector style transforms an ordinary red stapler into the vision of a futuristic motorcycle: sharp geometric lines, bold diagonal cuts, and clean color fields, where the contrast between metallic greys and vivid red emphasizes its sleek, aerodynamic volumes.
- **Goals:** Elevate everyday office objects · Surprise through ironic paradox · Highlight the value of design
- **Immagini:** slide `illustration-portfolio-05.webp` → **servono i file singoli**

### 7. Silvano Ferretti – copertine
- **Tipo:** Illustrazione editoriale / cover (edilizia)
- **Descrizione:** The three covers designed for Silvano Ferretti celebrate excellence in construction through a minimalist and material-driven visual language. Grainy gradients and textured surfaces evoke the qualities of concrete, stone, and other building materials, creating an almost tactile experience. Each cover focuses on a distinct area—interiors, exteriors, and swimming pools—using geometric forms and refined, desaturated color palettes. The contrast between the bold, structured logo and the tagline "dreams are handmade" ("i sogni sono fatti a mano") reinforces the brand's identity, expressing the balance between technical expertise, structural solidity, and meticulous craftsmanship.
- **Goals:** Communicate materiality through visual design · Evoke tactile sensations through grainy textures · Highlight products through tactile and visual perception
- **Immagini:** `assets/images/silvano-ferretti/` (3 copertine) + slide `illustration-portfolio-06.webp`

### 8. Various projects – Ghibellino 38, Basketball Tournament, Space Soda
- **Descrizione:** In the "Ghibellino 38" project, I reinterpret the landscape for a running event: the runner and the hilly road generate dynamic perspective force lines for T-shirts and postcards, using a high-contrast pop color triad. The sense of movement continues in "Basketball Tournament," where a powerful low-angle perspective monumentalizes the athletic figure and the intensity of the game. Finally, a pop-cosmic vision celebrates the energy of an effervescent bottle as it rises toward a starry sky, merging motion, symbolism, and visual impact into a cohesive narrative system across the three projects.
- **Goals:** Stylistic exercise · Strengthening synthesis skills · Search for visual identities
- **Immagini:** `assets/images/basketball/basketball-01.webp`, `assets/images/space-soda/space-soda-01.webp` + slide `illustration-portfolio-07.webp` (Ghibellino 38 → servono i file singoli)

### 9. Storyboard
- **Descrizione:** In addition to illustration projects, storyboard work was developed for animation, advertising, and illustrated publishing. The work focused on constructing visual sequences to define narrative rhythm, framing, character movement, and scene atmosphere. Through sketches and project boards, storyboarding supported the creative and production development phase, enabling the visualization of ideas before final execution. This activity allowed for a deeper understanding of cinematic language and visual synthesis, integrating illustration skills with attention to storytelling, continuity, and the communicative clarity of the visual narrative across different media and formats.
- **Goals:** Structuring visual storytelling · Visualizing ideas and scenes · Coordinating rhythm and framing
- **Immagini:** slide `illustration-portfolio-08.webp` → **servono le tavole singole**
